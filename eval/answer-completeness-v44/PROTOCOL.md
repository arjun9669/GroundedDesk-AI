## Owned grammar

- Purchase approval requests beginning with Who must approve / Who approves or
  What (written) approvals are required for, a named entity, purchase, one declared
  comparison operator, a nonnegative amount and optional three-letter unit label
  or unit/units. An optional same-policy written-approval question is accounted for;
  other trailing requested clauses produce a governed refusal.
- Operators: of/of exactly/exactly/equal to, above/over, at least, below/under and
  at most. Amounts have at most 12 integral and two fractional digits; integer
  hundredths avoid binary rounding at equality. Explicit units must agree.
- Bare named-entity ticket-order approver questions, optionally at a valid time.
  A matching unconditional named ticket-order approval relation can support the
  actor; departure, dispatch, generic order, acknowledgement or vehicle facts cannot.
- When was the large/big book/work/catalogue/manual at/in/of/from a named entity
  published. Support requires a unique explicit descriptor/date relation. Competing
  dates, non-designation or missing designation refuse. Named-title questions stay
  on the unchanged v43 path; this is not general language reference resolution.

The entity must match exactly one normalized filename identity. Existing v43 clock
ownership takes precedence, including unresolved and multi-facet forms. All other
outside-scope questions delegate unchanged to v43. The lexical Comparison, body
binding and clock helper exports are the exact v43 functions. No Stage B statistic/
retention/event or Stage C Comparison logic is implemented.

## Evidence and conservative failure boundary

Within an owned request, never invoke the previous answer producer or recover an
unsupported request through a prior cited answer. Require a unique complete source
relation. Purchase source rules must express approval from one/two simple role
phrases with their full written/range conditions. Explicit foreign entities cannot
supply authority. Conditional, negated, conflicting, duplicate or unparsed rules
refuse rather than silently losing conditions. Timing conditions cannot be absorbed
as role names. Role/source forms outside this bounded grammar can conservatively
refuse; this is a limitation requiring later preservation studies, not a claim of
general English support.

Finite source-body metadata, exact literal span identity, consistent document/text
identity, unique quotation location, current 620-character/three-source bounds and
original containing cosine anchors are required. Invalid metadata throws; absent
semantic support refuses. A finite fixture cosine cannot prove that a real model
or nonzero vector produced it: actual vectors and their audit belong to a future
separately frozen model runner. No threshold is changed and no score is fabricated.
