The Bellhaven Textile Museum relocated its collection in 1909. Its first conservation project finished in 1954; the second finished in 1982. A proposed closure in 2004 was rejected, and the museum remained open.

Decorative weaving panels line the upper stair rail. The entrance crown carries a bronze compass. The lower wall displays a painted harbour map.

The display cases use an argon fill because argon slows oxidation of the dyed fibres. The outer frame is aluminium and the shelf labels are paper.

Bellhaven Textile Museum visitor registers are retained for 18 months, unless an unresolved access complaint requires longer retention.
