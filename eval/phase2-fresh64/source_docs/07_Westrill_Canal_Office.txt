Westrill Canal Office moved its dispatch counter in 1917. Its first restoration was completed in 1965, and its second restoration was completed in 2003. A proposed closure in 1978 was rejected and did not occur.

Pera registers four permits per day. Sorin archives six maps per day. Pera checks two seals per day.

Westrill Canal Office toll receipts are retained for 10 months, unless an unresolved toll claim requires longer retention.

The Westrill Canal Office boarding packet contains a route plan, vessel register, radio authorization, and boarding pass.
