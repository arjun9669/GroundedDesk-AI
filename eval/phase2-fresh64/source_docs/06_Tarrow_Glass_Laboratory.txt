Tarrow Glass Laboratory uses dry nitrogen in its display vessels because dry nitrogen prevents moisture from clouding the coating. The support frames are steel and the labels are card.

Inspection emblems decorate the side balcony windows at Tarrow Glass Laboratory. The entrance arch has a blue crest, and the ceiling carries a brass vane.

The Tarrow Glass Laboratory winter average temperature is 12 degrees Celsius. Its highest monthly mean is 26 degrees Celsius in June, and its highest daily reading is 39 degrees Celsius. No absolute winter minimum is stated.

The Tarrow Glass Laboratory rig needs lubrication every 72 hours in normal conditions and every 36 hours in dusty conditions. Its inspection interval remains 18 hours in either condition.

Purchases above 7900 at Tarrow Glass Laboratory require written approval from both the Lab Curator and the Safety Warden.

Each Tarrow Glass Laboratory guided session admits at most 18 visitors.
