Kestwick Lens Workshop opened its public test bench in 1998. Its warehouse moved in 2013, and its roof was renewed in 2021.

Nera polishes five lenses per day. Olin labels eight crates per day. Nera checks two gauges per day.

The Kestwick Lens Workshop winter average temperature is 11 degrees Celsius. Its lowest recorded winter temperature is minus 6 degrees Celsius; the highest daily reading is 38 degrees Celsius.

Lubricate the Kestwick Lens Workshop rig every 96 hours in the clean bay and every 48 hours in the dusty bay. The inspection interval remains 24 hours in both bays.

Purchases above 6200 require written approval from both the Optics Lead and the Budget Steward at Kestwick Lens Workshop.

Kestwick Lens Workshop calibration logs are retained for 12 months, unless an unresolved calibration dispute requires longer retention.
