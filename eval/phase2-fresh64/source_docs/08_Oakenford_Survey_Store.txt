Oakenford Survey Store opened its public room in 1936. Its warehouse relocation took place in 1987, and its loading ramp was rebuilt in 2015.

The Oakenford Survey Store Control Ledger was published in 2014. A different book, Field Atlas, was published in 2020. Neither is designated the large book.

Each Oakenford Survey Store guided session admits at most 22 visitors.

The Oakenford Survey Store highest monthly mean temperature is 24 degrees Celsius in July. Its highest daily reading is 35 degrees Celsius and its lowest recorded winter reading is minus 4 degrees Celsius.

Oakenford Survey Store completed survey records are retained for 20 months, unless an unresolved audit dispute requires longer retention.
