Brindale Music Library opened its catalogue store in 1958. Its warehouse relocation took place in 1976. The main roof was rebuilt in 1999.

The Brindale Music Library catalogue Echoes of Reed was published in 1991. A different catalogue, Songs of Flint, was published in 2007. Neither is designated the large work.

Each Brindale Music Library guided session admits at most 28 visitors.

Brindale Music Library acknowledges a loan request within four business days. Acknowledgement does not promise confirmation of a loan.

Brindale Music Library loan receipts are retained for 9 months, unless an unresolved missing-instrument claim requires longer retention.

The highest monthly mean temperature in the Brindale Music Library store is 25 degrees Celsius in August. Its highest daily reading is 37 degrees Celsius.
