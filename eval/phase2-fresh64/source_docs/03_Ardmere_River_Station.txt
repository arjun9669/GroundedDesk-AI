Orders released before 12:35 depart on the current operating day. At or after 12:35, departure is the next operating day, unless a river inspection hold is still active.

Ardmere River Station cold samples must remain between 2 and 6 degrees Celsius during carriage.

Ardmere River Station dispatch records are retained for 16 months, unless an unresolved flood review requires longer retention.

Ardmere River Station acknowledges a carriage request within two operating days. Acknowledgement does not guarantee departure.
