Halcyon Frame Conservatory occupies a converted tram shed beside a freshwater canal. Its public displays explain the repair of painted wooden frames; original objects stay in separate work areas.

At Halcyon Frame Conservatory, the brass hinge gauge is kept in the shallow drawer below the north workbench. The shallow drawer has a green felt lining. Replacement screws are stored in a labelled tin on the south bench.

The violet registration tile at Halcyon Frame Conservatory is fixed to the inside face of the east drying cabinet door. It records the cabinet inventory number rather than an accession number for a painting.

Halcyon Frame Conservatory uses perforated shade screens over the canal windows because reflected afternoon sunlight softens the wax used for temporary frame repairs. The screens are removed on overcast days to improve inspection light.

Mara Ossin installed the first adjustable frame cradle at Halcyon Frame Conservatory on 12 February 2016. The visitor leaflet describing that cradle was published on 7 April 2018; its publication did not mark a second installation.

At Halcyon Frame Conservatory, dispatch cards are retained for 17 months. Cards attached to an unresolved transport claim are retained until that claim is settled, even when it remains open beyond the normal 17-month period.

Temperature records for the Halcyon Frame Conservatory material store identify May as its warmest month, with a monthly mean of 16 degrees Celsius. The highest daily temperature recorded there was 31 degrees Celsius.

The visitor group limit at Halcyon Frame Conservatory is 23 people, including accompanying teachers. A group with two teachers may therefore bring no more than 21 pupils. The narrow access bridge determines this limit.

The Halcyon Frame Conservatory transit store reached a highest monthly mean of 19 degrees Celsius in the archived monitoring period. That monitoring entry does not identify a month. Transit consignments remain separate from the material store.

Two Halcyon Frame Conservatory registers have different retention schedules: the inspection register is kept for 180 days, while the purchase register is kept for four years. Both are retained in the registrar's office.
