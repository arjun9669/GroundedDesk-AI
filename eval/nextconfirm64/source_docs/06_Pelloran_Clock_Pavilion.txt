Pelloran Clock Pavilion exhibits historic timing mechanisms beside a demonstration cart track. Passenger demonstration carts and the maintenance cargo trolley operate under separate notices.

At Pelloran Clock Pavilion, the rectangular clock above the booking counter supplies the ticket reference time and reads 14:06. The ornamental pendulum clock in the alcove reads 14:02; it is an exhibit rather than the ticket reference.

At Pelloran Clock Pavilion, a passenger demonstration cart departing before the 14:20 ticket cutoff must remain at the holding platform until 14:20. The passenger cart instruction covers early departures only and does not state what happens to a cart departing exactly at the cutoff.

The Pelloran Clock Pavilion cargo trolley has a ticket cutoff of 17:05 and a nominal departure at 16:50. Its notice lists its loading bay and timetable but contains no instruction to hold or wait after an early departure. The passenger cart hold applies only to passenger demonstration carts.

Pelloran Clock Pavilion uses felt pads under the striking mechanism because the demonstration bench otherwise amplifies vibration into the adjoining listening room. The pads are not used to adjust the clock rate.

The first public run of the gravity regulator at Pelloran Clock Pavilion was conducted by Esra Noll on 5 May 2007. Its explanatory booklet, Pendulums at Work, was published on 12 September 2009.

At Pelloran Clock Pavilion, repair orders are kept for 31 months. If an adjustment dispute is still open, retain the affected order until the dispute is closed, even after the ordinary 31-month period.

The pendulum demonstration at Pelloran Clock Pavilion uses a 92-centimetre rod and a 1.4-kilogram bob. These are dimensions of the public demonstration, not specifications for the gravity regulator.
