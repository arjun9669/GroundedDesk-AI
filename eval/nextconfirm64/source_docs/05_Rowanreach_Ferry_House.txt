Rowanreach Ferry House is the booking office for a short crossing to a river island. The passenger ferry and the cargo shuttle use different landing stages and separate boarding instructions.

Rowanreach Ferry House uses the square dial clock above the ticket hatch as the reference for passenger tickets. It shows 09:12. The round display clock beside the model boat shows 09:08 and is not the ticket-time reference.

At Rowanreach Ferry House, passenger ferry departures before the 09:25 ticket cutoff must wait until 09:25. Passengers already on the landing stage are covered by the same hold. This instruction concerns the passenger ferry rather than the cargo shuttle.

The Rowanreach Ferry House cargo shuttle has a 16:50 ticket cutoff. A cargo shuttle leaving before that cutoff must hold at the inner mooring until 16:50. Its nominal loading time is 16:35.

Rowanreach Ferry House paints a broad white stripe on each ramp edge because dark wet timber is difficult to distinguish from the water at dusk. The stripes identify the edge and are not water-depth markings.

At Rowanreach Ferry House, Linor Cask fitted the first hinged ticket hatch on 17 June 2010. The island landing steps were replaced on 8 October 2012; the later work did not change the hatch installation date.

Rowanreach Ferry House keeps crossing manifests for 13 months. Manifests named in an open landing dispute remain on file until the dispute is resolved, including disputes continuing beyond the normal period.

The passenger ferry booked through Rowanreach Ferry House carries at most 34 passengers. Crew members are not included in that passenger limit. The separate cargo shuttle does not offer passenger places.
