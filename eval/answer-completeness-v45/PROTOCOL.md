## Bounded owned requests

- What is the minimum/lowest (optionally recorded/winter) or absolute minimum
  temperature at/in/of/for an explicit named document entity. A requested winter
  metric cannot use a non-winter metric. Recorded and absolute requests keep their
  specific label kinds; generic minima require a unique qualifying value. Preserve
  literal minus/negative signs and Celsius/Fahrenheit units. Do not substitute
  averages, monthly means or daily highs; those question types delegate unchanged.
- How long are/is a record subject kept/retained, optionally including an exception
  or asking which/what exception applies. Normalize an exact entity prefix on both
  question and source, retain the entire literal duration/exception and require a
  unique policy for the same record type. Acknowledgement is not retention.
- When was the first/second/third/fourth/fifth named-entity restoration, overhaul,
  renovation, relocation, opening or rebuilding completed/finished; or when did it
  take place/occur. Each explicit coordinated ordinal/date clause is a separate
  literal span. Require a unique affirmative matching event/ordinal/date.

Only declared source forms are structurally supported. Unknown trailing facets,
competing values, denied/qualified relations or ambiguous source identities refuse
inside governed scope; there is no unrelated-prior-answer fallback. Stage A safety
contracts and existing v43 clock ownership take precedence. Also compounds, other
metrics/nonordinal events and lexical Comparison remain exact prior delegation.

## Evidence bounds and limitations

Every body requires finite in-range cosine metadata, consistent document/source
identity and an exact literal offset. Retained quotes are unique and at most 620
characters, with sequential citations inside the existing three-source limit.
The containing body's original cosine/anchor remains explicit when a clause is
selected; do not claim that the selected clause was separately embedded.

Local Its links require adjacent same-paragraph entity scope, with competing proper
names resetting that scope; minimum pronouns also require a preceding temperature
statement. Paragraph boundaries include LF and CRLF. This heuristic is intentionally
bounded, not general coreference. No cross-paragraph or foreign-entity date/value
can supply support. Source grammar outside these forms may refuse rather than
relaxing a source or provenance constraint. No threshold or resource cap is lowered.
