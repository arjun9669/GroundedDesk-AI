## Minimum request, metric and source grammar

Own `What is [the] LABEL at/in/of/for TARGET`. LABEL is minimum/lowest, optionally
recorded/winter, or absolute [recorded] minimum [recorded] [winter] temperature.
Explicit recorded and absolute labels stay distinct. A generic minimum/lowest
requires one unique qualifying label/value. Winter and nonwinter cannot substitute.

TARGET resolves to one established entity (filename or existing explicit policy
scope) plus an optional separately parsed location. Locations are store/room/gallery/
bay/vault, optionally material/prop/alloy/sample/cold/warm/storage/archive/test/clean/
dusty/upper/lower. A requested subentity must be literally evidenced in the source,
not guessed from a filename or stripped as an arbitrary suffix. Exact identity and
unique complete-token aliases reuse v47's resolver; competing aliases/owners refuse.

Source forms: `[The] ENTITY [LOCATION] LABEL is/was/of VALUE UNIT`,
`[The] LABEL in/at/of/for ENTITY [LOCATION] is/was/of VALUE UNIT`,
`Its LABEL is/was/of VALUE UNIT`, or bare `[The] LABEL is/was/of VALUE UNIT`.
Allow declared possessive entity prefixes. Context metrics are winter average,
highest monthly mean/average and highest [recorded] daily temperature. A local Its
or subsequent bare label needs the immediately preceding parsed metric context in
the same policy block; a first bare label may use filename scope without a location.
Unknown/foreign antecedents and paragraph/policy crossings do not establish scope.

A value is a bounded decimal with optional minus/negative/ASCII-minus/Unicode-minus,
Celsius/Fahrenheit (degree/degrees) or °C/°F units. Preserve literal signs, units and
source offsets. Split only at original semicolons or explicit coordinated new metric
clauses; never fabricate punctuation. Conflicting, negated, qualified, missing or
uninterpreted matching minimum records refuse; other metrics remain distinct.
Minimum metadata is never inferred from an average, daily reading or another room.

## Purchase approval request and source grammar

Own `Who must approve/approves [in writing] [a/an/the] ENTITY purchase OP AMOUNT
[UNIT]`, or `What [written] approval(s) is/are required for [a/an/the] ENTITY purchase
OP AMOUNT [UNIT]`. Optional final `and is written approval required/approval written`
is consumed as a writing facet. Unknown extra clauses and malformed owned forms
refuse; the writing phrase is not absorbed into entity identity.

OP: above/over/exceeding, below/under, at least/at most, of/of exactly/exactly/equal to.
Normalize exceeding to strict above and reuse unchanged v44 range containment.
Amounts are at most12 integer digits and at most2 decimals. Units: credit/credits,
unit/units, or literal three-letter codes (reserved grammar words excluded). Missing
unit matches only missing unit; no currency/unit conversion or implicit exemption.

Source: `Purchases OP AMOUNT [UNIT] [at ENTITY] require [written] approval from
ROLE [and ROLE] [at ENTITY]`, optionally prefixed by `At ENTITY,` where that entity
is already established. One or two distinct literal roles are supported, optional
both for two roles; qualified, denied, conditional or unparsed role clauses refuse.
Explicit source entity must match the requested document entity. More than one
same-unit rule overlapping any part of the asked range is ambiguous, even if one
rule alone covers it. One unique overlapping rule must cover the entire asked range
and establish writing if requested. Equality outside a strict above rule refuses.
Ticket departure evidence can never supply approval actors.
