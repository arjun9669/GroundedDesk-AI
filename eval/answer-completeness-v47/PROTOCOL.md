## Ownership and request grammar

Use unchanged v43 for the default Ask and exact Comparison/helper exports. Its
clock-owned requests, including unresolved ones, remain authoritative. Questions
containing the existing `Also,` compound delimiter delegate whole to v43; no
partial retention parse or new compound-completeness claim is made.

Own `How long are/is SUBJECT kept/retained`, optional at/in ENTITY location before
or after the predicate and optional `normally`. The remainder must be empty or one
of: `including [the/any] exception`, `[DESCRIPTOR] exception`, `exception for
DESCRIPTOR`, `and what/which exception applies`, `and when must they/it be kept
longer`, `and what happens if/while CONDITION`, or `and what claim/dispute/review/
investigation/complaint/case/hold can extend that time`. A descriptor/condition has
one condition noun, up to four modifiers, optional open/unresolved/active/pending/
ongoing status, or `is [still] STATUS`. State open/unresolved/active/pending/ongoing
is explicitly normalized as ongoing, not inferred from an unrelated source.
Unknown conjunctions/facets, conflicting entity positions, missing subjects and
questions over600 characters are terminal uncited refusals within ownership.

Record heads: record, receipt, slip, log, register, form, report, file, card,
catalogue, notebook, sheet, ticket and their declared simple plurals. Unprefixed request
subjects have up to four modifiers drawn from the declared generic list in
`scope.mjs`; this includes record-domain nouns and participles, never case IDs or
proper names. Explicitly scoped subjects may use up to five literal word modifiers
with those heads. Matching is case-independent and retains all subtype words.
This is a closed record grammar, not arbitrary named-entity understanding.

## Identity and source grammar

Establish identity through a normalized filename entity (numeric prefix/extension
removed) or explicit `At ENTITY, SUBJECT are/is ...` policy introduction. A unique
complete-token prefix of an established entity may be an alias; generic aliases
such as guide/current/document are not sufficient. Exact/full matches take priority.
Record the real document ID/filename and literal scope position. Conflicting global
aliases or same-entity owners refuse; retrieval rank never chooses identity.
Unknown named prefixes do not become generic subjects by changing capitalization.
Bare source labels may retain up to five literal modifiers under document scope;
they cannot establish a foreign entity or strip an unknown prefix. A sole-file
fallback is not used. Unprefixed typed subjects require one unique
matching policy across all supplied documents. Explicitly scoped subjects retain
full entity proof even when a historical filename is unrelated to its policy entity.

A base source is `SUBJECT are/is kept/retained for DURATION`, optionally prefixed
by `At ENTITY,` or an established entity. Duration is a nonnegative decimal or
one..twelve followed by day/week/month/year (plural allowed), with optional
business/operating/calendar/service/working modifier. No unit conversion is inferred.
Keep all conditions, even if the question asks only a normal duration.

Support an inline `unless/except [that/when] CONDITION requires longer retention`
or `requires retention until the CONDITION-NOUN is settled/closed/resolved/...`;
also adjacent same-policy forms: `If/While CONDITION, keep/retain [the] affected SUBJECT
until the CONDITION-NOUN ...`, `SUBJECT under CONDITION are/is kept until ...`,
and `CONDITION extends retention of [the] affected SUBJECT until ...`.
An optional `even after the DURATION period` must agree with the base duration.
The full condition and action must parse; no abbreviated exception is invented.
Condition modifiers requested in the question must be present in the source;
claim/dispute/review/etc are distinct. Affected generic record-head references
are resolved only to the unique immediately preceding policy subject. Other record
types, policy numbers, Markdown headings, blank paragraphs or unrelated sentences
cannot supply a borrowed extension. Unknown/denied/conflicting retention clauses
or duplicate policy identities refuse. Acknowledgement is a different relation.

## Evidence and prior signature preservation

Body metadata must be finite, literal and internally consistent, within the
existing15-document/512-body/180,000-character-per-document limits. These fields
do not themselves prove real embeddings; future model execution audits actual
corresponding384D vectors. No scores or thresholds in reused v43 paths change.

Construct one unique contiguous quote covering the base and all adjacent parsed
extensions in its policy block. At most620 literal characters, with original
normalized-body offsets and no punctuation fabrication or fragment concatenation.
Bind using unchanged `bindFacetSource`, retaining the real containing body ID,
cosine and explicit anchor. Do not claim a new embedding for a selected subclause.

Only after a complete unique policy is established may a prior v43 answer be
considered. Retain its answer/filename/quote signature only if it has exactly one
properly cited literal source, wholly in the same policy block, covering the full
validated policy/conditions. Rebind actual source-body metadata; never reuse an
unrelated acknowledgement or partial-duration answer. Unsupported scope does not
call the old answer first. Zero allowed citations refuses; invalid limits/metadata
throw engineering errors rather than becoming semantic passes.
