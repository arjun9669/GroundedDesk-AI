## Decision

A location-specific request requires affirmative source evidence tying the requested
metric/value/month to that location in each selected document. A filename establishes
document identity; it does not establish that an unqualified facility temperature
was measured in its store. Do not infer store from laboratory, borrow another
room's value, use a daily/winter figure as a monthly figure, or discard a requested
location to produce an answer. Incomplete or conflicting evidence returns an
uncited incomplete comparison. An unqualified facility question may use facility
values; that is a different question with a different evidence requirement.

Bounded local Its can inherit an explicit same-document/location metric antecedent
within one policy block. It cannot cross an unrelated statement, paragraph or
conflicting room. Preserve signed quantity, unit, month, selected-pair identity,
input citation order and literal contiguous offsets. This documents the existing
v50/v49 contract; it is not a candidate behavior change or a new inference rule.

## Original evidence assessment

Fresh64 FC61 asks for highest monthly mean store temperatures and months at
Brindale Music Library and Tarrow Glass Laboratory. Brindale expressly identifies
its store. Tarrow supplies a monthly mean 26 degrees Celsius/June within a laboratory
temperature paragraph, but the complete source never identifies that as a store
measurement. The monthly number/month is supported; the store attribution is not.

The original frozen rubric expects the two number/month pairs and records v50's
uncited refusal as native FAIL. Preserve that question, source, key and outcome
unchanged. This assessment explains the conflict between requested scope and
available evidence; it does not regrade the case, remove it from 64, change the
required 64/64, or turn63/64 into a passing confirmation.
