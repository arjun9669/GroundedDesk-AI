## Implemented contracts

to unchanged v47, retaining package-1 scope/retention. V43 clock ownership and whole
Also-compound routing remain authoritative; Comparison/helpers keep exact original
export identities. No new Comparison implementation is included.

[Minimum binding](../eval/answer-completeness-v48/minimum.mjs) separates established
document identity from a literal room/store subentity. It handles leading named
sources and temperature-in-location forms, with bounded same-block metric context
for Its/bare clauses. Unknown or competing locations, foreign/distant antecedents,
wrong metrics and ambiguous/qualified/negated values refuse. Explicit recorded and
absolute labels stay distinct; generic minima require unique support. Literal
minus/negative signs and Celsius/Fahrenheit units are preserved. No average or
daily value can substitute for a minimum.

[Approval binding](../eval/answer-completeness-v48/approval.mjs) consumes fronted or
trailing writing facets, credit singular/plural and declared literal units/codes.
Exceeding canonicalizes to strict above using unchanged v44 range containment.
Exact integer-cent overlap checks reject conflicting partial-range rules, even
when another rule covers the requested range. Equality outside a strict rule,
missing/mismatched units, unknown extra clauses, incomplete/conditional roles or
unestablished writing refuse. Full source roles and writing conditions are retained.
Existing v44 ticket-actor and ambiguous-publication contracts are reused unchanged;
departure evidence cannot become an approval actor. Named titles remain on v47.

[Common evidence binding](../eval/answer-completeness-v48/common.mjs) uses unchanged
v47 body/identity validation and v43 literal source helpers. Supported prior display
signatures may be retained only after full new-contract validation. Unsupported
owned requests never call the prior first. Quotes remain unique contiguous spans
<=620 characters, with actual supplied body/cosine anchors and sequential citations.
No subclause embedding or similarity is invented; synthetic fixture metadata is
not a real MiniLM measurement. Limits and thresholds on reused paths are unchanged.

The [pre-test protocol](../eval/answer-completeness-v48/PROTOCOL.md) specifies exact
owned question/source forms, units, location vocabulary, evidence and refusal bounds.
This is bounded parsing, not unrestricted entity/coreference or policy comprehension.
