# Hearth32 frozen scoring rubric

Unchanged Morrow32 displayed-evidence criteria. Score baseline, fixed, section, paired_fixed and paired_section separately. Capture all32 before grading.

Supported PASS: every frozen fact group appears literally in a displayed quotation in the expected file/context; all citations literal/relevant with valid sequential citation numbers; no contradiction. PARTIAL: at least one requested supported group, but missing groups or irrelevant/cross-context citations. FAIL: no supported group, invalid provenance/integrity, contradiction or supported refusal. Expanded retrieval context or unstated inference earns no credit.

Unsupported PASS: explicit no-evidence refusal or ambiguity clarification, zero citations. Otherwise FAIL, including literal negative/nonresponsive answers under this strict protocol. Record fabricated affirmative claims separately from negative or nonresponsive evidence. Report false acceptance as unsupported non-refusal; false refusal as supported explicit refusal. Supported nonresponsive answers are FAIL but are not counted as unsupported false acceptances.

Expected-context containment and direct group coverage are proxies, not general entailment. Record provenance, paragraph precision, relevance, completeness and number integrity separately. Preserve original automated grades. Post-seal assistant review can downgrade an explicit contradiction and must preserve both versions; it cannot infer missing credit or relax thresholds. Overall gate BOTH variants:32/32 PASS, zero false acceptance/refusal, all citations literal/relevant, no production or paired-v31 PASS loss. Do not pool with643 exposed regressions or claim external independent accuracy.
