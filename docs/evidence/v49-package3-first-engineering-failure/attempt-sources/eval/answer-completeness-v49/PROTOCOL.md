## Question grammar

Own Compare questions <=600 characters, with optional leading the and final ./?.
- Event: `OBJECT EVENT years/dates for A and B`, optionally ordinal first–fifth;
  or `A OBJECT EVENT year/date with B OBJECT EVENT year/date` (plural also allowed).
  Event: relocation/move, restoration, renovation, opening, rebuilding. Objects are
  independently parsed bounded1–4 word literal phrases, not merged across sides.
- Monthly: `highest monthly mean/average temperatures and [their] months for A and B`;
  or `highest monthly mean/average [LOCATION] temperatures at/in A and B, including
  [their] months`. Mean and average are distinct literal labels. LOCATION is
  store/room/gallery/bay/vault, optionally material/prop/alloy/sample/cold/warm/storage/
  archive/test/clean/dusty/upper/lower. An explicit location must be evidenced.
- Departure: `departure at [the/each] stated release cutoff for A orders and B
  ticket orders, including [their] holds`; each side independently allows orders or
  ticket orders. Also reuse unchanged v46 complete before/after cutoff/day/hold
  grammar for its original shared relation form, retaining both branches/holds.

Resolve full filename entities and unique complete-token aliases across the two
selected files; exact full identities take precedence. Optional articles do not
create identities. Both sides must resolve to the two distinct original documents,
without dropping third identities, unknown suffixes or additional requested facets.
Preserve input document/citation order even if question order is reversed.

## Source binding

Normalize body newlines and horizontal whitespace exactly as inherited Comparison;
offset/quote identity refers to that body, not raw upload bytes. At most180,000
characters per named document, distinct nonempty IDs and filename entities.

Event source: `[The] [ENTITY] [ordinal] OBJECT was moved/relocated/opened/rebuilt
in/during YEAR`; restoration/renovation use `was completed`. Also `ENTITY
moved/relocated/opened/rebuilt its OBJECT in/during YEAR` and `OBJECT EVENT took
place/occurred/was completed in/during YEAR`. Local Its needs an immediately preceding
parsed, scoped affirmative event in the same block. Include its literal contiguous
antecedent in the quote; never borrow foreign scope, unrelated statements or another
paragraph. Bare literal matching object/event statements can use filename scope.
No proposed, denied, qualified, contradictory or multiple matching date supplies support.

Monthly: `[The] [ENTITY] [LOCATION] highest monthly mean/average [temperature]
is/was QUANTITY UNIT in MONTH`, also month-before-value order; or temperature
`in/at [the] ENTITY [LOCATION] is/was VALUE UNIT in MONTH`. A bare full metric may
use filename scope only without an explicit requested location. Its metric inherits
only an immediately preceding parsed temperature context within the same block:
winter average, monthly mean/average or highest daily temperature/reading, explicitly
bound to the same document/location. Quote includes that contiguous antecedent.
Signed minus/negative/ASCII/Unicode values and Celsius/Fahrenheit/°C/°F stay literal.
A winter average/daily high cannot fill a monthly facet. Missing month, unknown
location, false antecedent or duplicate/conflicting monthly relation refuses.
Unrelated missing minimum does not invalidate an independently supported monthly metric.

Cutoff-equality: `[Ticket] orders released at or after HH:MM depart on the next
operating/service/business/working/collection day, unless HOLD is/remains [still]
active/in effect`. Bare `At or after HH:MM, departure is [the] next DAY, unless...`
is orders-only. A hold allows1–4 literal modifier words followed by hold. Equality
is explicit; exclusive after/above is insufficient. Require exactly one matching
departure-after rule, full literal hold and exact requested order type. Each side
retains its own cutoff/day/hold. The at-cutoff grammar does not require a before
branch or a hold on that unrequested branch. More general time/hold/coreference
language remains unsupported rather than inferred.

Split only existing semicolons and explicit `and its/the` coordination; preserve
contiguous source bytes and punctuation. Each source must uniquely contain its
quote <=620 characters in one original block; two sequential citations or none.
Public Comparison metadata shape is preserved through compareDocuments; its raw
lexical filler never establishes completeness. No invented cosine fields.
