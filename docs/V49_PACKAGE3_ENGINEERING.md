## Implementation and limits

The [new wrapper](../eval/answer-completeness-v49/coverage.mjs) retains v48 fallback,
package1 retention, package2 minimum/written-approval and ticket/publication paths.
Only ordinal-event plans reuse the unchanged v45 planner/answer contract, preserving
its actual body/cosine-anchor binding. Clock and whole Also-compound ownership stay
unchanged. No older candidate or production import changes.

[Comparison contracts](../eval/answer-completeness-v49/comparison.mjs) bind each
original selected document independently to its event/object/date, cutoff/day/hold
or monthly temperature/month. Different event objects and move/relocation aliases
are supported. Bounded local Its quotes retain their literal antecedent; foreign,
ambiguous, unrelated or distant antecedents refuse. Each requested location, order
type, day unit, month and hold must be evidenced. Equality requires an explicit
inclusive cutoff; a before branch is unnecessary when only equality is requested.
Original complete before/after cutoff grammar reuses unchanged v46 implementation.

Both sides must be complete or the owned result is uncited and incomplete. Exact
selected IDs, filenames and input citation order remain intact, including reversed
question order. Quotes remain unique, contiguous, <=620 characters with normalized
body offsets. Comparison remains lexical with no invented cosines. Unsupported
retention, maintenance, acknowledgement, packet and capacity families delegate
exactly to the previous candidate. The [protocol](../eval/answer-completeness-v49/PROTOCOL.md)
declares bounded forms; broader natural language is not claimed. Mean and average
remain distinct labels. An explicitly requested location cannot be inferred from
a nearby value or an unscoped document. These limits may still affect frozen cases;
no historical case was replayed to check them.
