          How your files and answers are handled
          
        
          02 / UNDER THE HOOD
          How GroundedDesk works
          
        
          
          
          
            
          RETRIEVAL PIPELINE
          
            
          01 · Extract→02 · Chunk→03 · Rank→04 · Cite
          
            
          Documents are processed inside your browser. Keyword mode uses BM25-style lexical passage ranking, while optional hybrid retrieval fuses local MiniLM embeddings with keyword ranking. Extractive answers are verbatim citations by default. Experimental 3.0 uses a separate local Qwen 0.5B WebGPU language model to draft cited sentences from retrieved excerpts; source-ID checks cannot guarantee factual correctness, and invalid outputs fall back to extracts. Public model/library downloads need internet access, but document text is not sent to an inference server. Relevance and test-set scores are not production accuracy claims.
          
          
          
          
          ENGINEERING NOTES
          • Private by default — no shared document storage
          • Inspectable — ranking source and tests in repository
          • Honest limitations — keyword matching is not semantic reasoning
          • PDF dependency — PDF.js library loaded from a third-party CDN
          • Optional semantics — MiniLM model downloaded publicly and inferred on-device
          • Experimental generation — Qwen 0.5B on WebGPU requires additional memory/download; its citations are structurally checked, not fact-verified

          A LITTLE HELP TO GET STARTED
          Your first grounded answer
          
  
          Step 1 of 4
          
  
          
    
          01
          Start with your documents.
          Add PDF, TXT or Markdown files in the source library, or try the fictional examples already loaded. Each file can be up to 5 MB. PDFs need searchable text; scanned pages need OCR outside this app.
          Files are cleared when this tab closes or reloads unless you explicitly save a snapshot using Keep this workspace. Saved files stay in this browser until you clear the snapshot or browser data.
          
    
          02
          Choose the right tool.
          Ask a question and read quoted answers with numbered citations.
          Choose two files and a topic to inspect their excerpts side by side.
          Gather passages across files, or review numbered policy sections and checklists.
          Compare and Research use keyword search. Their excerpts still need your review.
          
    
          03
          Ask clearly. Start simple.
          Try a specific question, such as “Who must approve a release?” Use names and terms from your files when you can.
          Fast search is ready immediately.
          Optional Local AI search downloads a free model to help find related wording in Ask. It runs on your device and needs rebuilding after you change files. It does not make every answer correct.
          Experimental answer drafts are separate and start off.
          
    
          04
          Follow the evidence.
          Read the quotation, filename and page or line reference. Click a numbered citation to find its evidence card. For PDFs, Open cited PDF page shows the original page; highlights are approximate.
          An answer without enough evidence may be refused. A relevant-looking citation is not proof that the answer is complete or correct.
          Use Copy report or Save report to keep the answer and its sources.
          
  
          
  
          
  
          The guide remembers only that you closed it. Save files separately with Keep this workspace; questions are not saved.
